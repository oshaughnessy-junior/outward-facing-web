## Research collaborations

We welcome conversations about gravitational-wave astrophysics, scientific inference and simulation, accelerated computing, and accountable AI research workflows. Start with the [research overview]({{ '/research/' | relative_url }}) for primary papers and inspectable software.

For a useful first discussion, include the scientific question, a relevant paper or repository, and the proposed contribution. University, national-laboratory, AI-lab, and other research partners can reach Richard through his RIT directory entry above.
